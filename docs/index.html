Everything you love about the big streaming apps. Running on your own library. With a player that plays anything.

Home

It looks like the apps you already love.

A rotating banner, Continue Watching, rows that glow with the colours of the artwork. Your Jellyfin, finally dressed for the occasion.

Search

Find anything in a keystroke.

Filter by type, genre or year and see results as posters. The titles you opened last are waiting when you come back.

Details

Every title gets its moment.

Big artwork, the whole cast in circles, seasons and episodes, trailers, and full media info for the curious.

Library

Know what you've watched, at a glance.

A small check marks what you finished and a thin bar shows how far you got. Favourites are one heart away.

On your phone

Your pocket cinema.

The same calm look on Android, with a glass navigation bar, gestures for brightness and volume, and a battery saver that eases off when the phone runs warm.

What's inside

Small details. Big difference.

A player that plays everything

Powered by libmpv. Almost any video, audio and subtitle format, including PGS and styled ASS subtitles, and Direct Play whenever your server allows it.

Smart Fit

Black bars baked into a video are removed on their own, only when it truly makes the picture bigger.

Skip Intro & Up Next

One tap past the opening credits, and the next episode starts by itself.

Discover

Browse what's popular and request what your server doesn't have, through Jellyseerr. Sign in with your Jellyfin account.

Gentle on the battery

Hardware decoding, light effects, and a saver mode when the phone gets warm.

Picture-in-picture

Keep watching while you browse. Mini player on Windows, PiP on Android.

Profiles & history

Several profiles and servers, watch history and stats, and a home and a remote address for the same server.

Private by design

Your password goes only to your own server. No accounts, no analytics, no tracking. Vyneo never hosts or provides any media.

Updates that stay out of your way

New versions download quietly in the background. When one is ready you choose: restart now, or later.

For server owners

See who's watching. Right now.

Pro unlocks Server admin: live sessions, a readable activity log of who played what and who actually finished it, library tools, and restart or shut down for your server.

Free, for good

Made by one person, in the evenings.

Watching, browsing and requesting are free for everyone. If Vyneo makes your movie nights nicer, a small gift keeps it going and earns you a badge.
