A Jellyfin app for Android that is easy on the eyes and the battery

Vyneo brings your Jellyfin library to your phone or tablet with a fast, polished interface and a proper video player.

Download Vyneo (free)See everything Vyneo does

What you get on Android

  • A real video player (libmpv) with hardware decoding, PGS and styled ASS subtitles, an audio and subtitle picker, and Direct Play first.
  • Gestures: swipe for brightness and volume, double-tap to skip.
  • Glass navigation bar and smooth transitions between screens.
  • Battery-saver mode. When the phone is in battery saver or gets warm, Vyneo drops the heavy effects.
  • Discover through Jellyseerr, signing in with your Jellyfin account.
  • Picture-in-picture, Chromecast, several profiles and servers, and a home and remote address for the same server.
  • Favourites, watch history and stats.

Requirements

  • Android 8.0 or newer (phones and tablets)
  • A Jellyfin server, 10.9 or newer is best

Installing

Vyneo is not on Google Play yet. Download Vyneo-Android (an .apk file) from the latest release, open it, and allow installs from your browser or files app when Android asks. Android TV is not supported yet; a redesign for the big screen is planned.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Jellyfin app Vyneo free?

Yes. Everything for watching, browsing and requesting is free.

Is it on Google Play?

Not yet. You install the APK from the GitHub releases page.

Does it work on Android TV?

Not yet. A full redesign for TVs is planned.

Does it drain the battery?

It uses hardware video decoding and light effects, and switches to a saver mode when the phone is in battery saver or running warm.

Vyneo is a third-party app and is not affiliated with the Jellyfin project. It does not host or provide any media; it plays what is on your own Jellyfin server.
